## p073 — The Commands List (frequency-string syntax)

The whole **Frequencies field** mini-language, which is what every database row's
"frequency list" column is written in. Only the tokens that matter for reading preset
strings:

| Token | Meaning | Literal definition from the page |
|---|---|---|
| `xxx-yyy` | Frequency Sweep | "how you enter a sweep between two frequencies — *xxx* stands for one frequency, and *yyy* for the other." **Ascending = lower first** (`1234-5678`); **descending = higher first** (`5678-1234`) |
| `=xxx` | Custom Dwell | "For any frequencies, you can specify different dwells from the default. Just type an equal sign … followed by the dwell in seconds." |
| `W`/`w` | Waveform, Out 1 / Out 2 | number table below |
| `G` | Gate | "`G` followed by 1 to turn gating on, and `G` followed by 0 to turn it off" |
| `A` / `a` | Amplitude Out 1 / Out 2 | "peak to peak voltage of Out 1 (or 2) you wish to use" |
| `Lx` | Light Wavelength | wavelength in nanometers; converted to "the highest subharmonic frequency your generator is set up to transmit" |
| `Mx` | MW Factor | Monoisotopic Molecular Weight (g/mol) → frequency |
| `O` / `o` | Offset | "`upper-case O` … the value desired as a **percentage of Amplitude** rather than a voltage. For negative offset type lower-case `o`" |
| `Px` | Phase Angle | value in degrees |
| `F` / `C` | Factor / Constant | "Out 2 = (Out 1 × *Factor*) + *Constant*" |
| `T+` / `T-` | Tissue Factor On / Off | |
| `[...]` | Chemical formula | "converts the formula to a mono-isotopic mass frequency" — e.g. `[C860H1353N227O255S9]` |

**Waveform number table (W = Out 1, w = Out 2):**

| # | Wave | # | Wave |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sine wave | 7 | Damped Square |
| 2 | Square wave | 8 | Sine H-Bomb |
| 3 | Sawtooth wave | 9 | Square H-Bomb |
| 4 | Inverted sawtooth | 10 | User defined #1 |
| 5 | Triangle wave | 11 | User defined #2 |
| 6 | Damped Sinusoidal | | |

> **Why the skill cares:** the `(CUST, 100.5, X-32 ...)` prefixes seen in p78/p84/p95
> are these tokens in serialised form. "X-32" = triangle wave, "X=600" = 600-second
> dwell, "A09" = 9 V Out 1. Parsing a preset's frequency string is exactly this table.

## p074 — Commands List continued + worked example

Continues the command vocabulary (see p073) and adds the factorized encodings:

- `Bx` (Base Pairs Factor), `BCx` (Circular Base Pairs), `BLx` (Linear Base Pairs) — each
  converts a genome parameter to a frequency.
- `Fx` **Factor** and `Cx` **Constant** implement `Out 2 = (Out 1 × Factor) + Constant`.
  `CLx` can be used as a Constant for Out 2; `CMx` as a Molecular Weight Constant;
  `CBx` as a Base Pair Constant.

**The canonical worked example (useful as a test vector for any parser):**

- Frequencies 100, 200, 300, 400, 450, 600.
- Desired: 100 and 200 at the 3-minute default dwell; 300 for 10 minutes; square wave,
  Amplitude 9 V, Offset 0.
  `100 W2 A09 O00,200,300=600,` — where `300=600` is a **10-minute (600 s) dwell**
  and `O00` is offset 0.
- Then 400 to 450 ascending over 30 minutes, sine:
  `400-450=1800 W1,` — the **whole sweep gets its own dwell** of 1800 s.
- Then 600 again, square, 5 V, +2.5 V offset (= 50 % of Amplitude):
  `600 W2 A05 O50,`

Full assembled string:
`100 W2 A09 O00,200,300=600,400-450=1800 W1,600 W2 A05 O50,`

> Note the ordering rule implied: waveform/amplitude/offset codes are **per frequency
> item**, not global.

## p077 — Carrier vs Modulation Sweep: the two-minute distinction

Read this whole page as the reasoning for **why a sweep is or isn't a good recommendation**.

- "A carrier is a fixed frequency. More frequencies can be created if this frequency is
  turned on and off very rapidly. Another word for this is '**modulation**'."
- Modulation sets the **spacing between sidebands**; the guide uses the *inverted hair
  comb* metaphor: teeth = sideband frequencies, dip the teeth in ink and press onto
  white paper → a line of black dots = the frequency coverage.
- **The disadvantage of modulation sweeps, stated explicitly:**
  > "Each frequency is produced for **eight seconds or less**. Dr. Rife applied his
  > frequencies for three minutes. This may explain why such sweeps have not produced
  > 'spontaneous remissions' as they should."
- **Spooky2's Carrier Sweep, by contrast:**
  > "The Spooky² Carrier Sweep applies each frequency **once**, but it **pounds on it
  > thoroughly for at least three minutes**. The carrier will receive most of the power,
  > so choose a frequency in the **center** of your target range."
- `Frequency Tolerance`: "Royal Rife specified 1/4 of 1%, or **0.25%**. Spooky² sets the
  default to **.025%**." (Note: the Carrier Sweep dialog default on p76 is `.025`.)
- **Duration warning:** "When creating a Carrier Sweep, keep an eye on the *Sweep
  Duration*. This is the time required to complete the sweep. **Often this can extend to
  many hours** if the settings are altered without care."
- "Spooky² can also perform conventional Modulation Sweeps if required."

## p079 — Spectrum Sweep field definitions + required Settings

- **Frequency Application Time**: "the number of seconds to stay on each frequency of the sweep."
- **Wave Cycle Multiplier**: "the number of simultaneous frequencies applied around a
  center frequency. The larger this number, the more simultaneous frequencies.
  **However, the total voltage per frequency shrinks as you increase this number.**"
- **Frequency Hits Per Sweep**: "the number of times each frequency is targeted per sweep."
- **Spectrum Sweep Info** updates only after you "select a *Sweep Type*" — i.e. the info
  pane is stale until you re-click a radio.
- **Database Entry View**: "you see your custom Spectrum sweep **exactly as it will appear
  in your database**."

**Required Settings for `Single Spectrum Sweep` (Settings tab):**
1. Click *Restore Defaults*.
2. In *Signal Settings*, set **Out 1 and Out 2 amplitude to 20**.
3. In *Waveform Setup*: Out 1 waveform = **Sine wave**, Out 2 = **Invert**, and in
   *Output Control* set `Out 2 = (Out 1 X 1) + 0`.
4. Use the 4th field of the *Database Entry View* to set the **WCM** and **Spectrum** field
   of the Out 1 Sine waveform in *Waveform Setup*.

> Safety-adjacent instruction quoted: **"Click Restore Defaults."** is step 1 of all three
> recipes — an incomplete Settings tab silently breaks the sweep.
