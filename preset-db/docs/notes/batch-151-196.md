## p185
**Spectrum amplitude dilution — a hard practical limit on recommending large Spectrum presets.**
> "Spectrum is a mathematical way to make one 'parent' static or moving frequency produce up to
> **1,024 'child' frequencies simultaneously**, spread equally above and below that frequency.
> While this is an amazing feat, you must never forget that **a generator's available amplitude is
> divided between all the frequencies it's currently transmitting.**"

> "**The Spooky2-XM and GeneratorX Pro generators operate at a maximum of 20 volts.**"

> "So if you build a Spectrum that creates 1,024 frequencies at the same time, the amplitude of

> in Remote Mode it's certain that it would be **no good in Contact Mode, which needs far higher
> voltages to penetrate the skin**."

**Two stated remedies:** reduce the number of frequencies transmitted; or use **Spooky
Central/Plasma**.

**Spectrum vs wobble — a real behavioural difference:**
> "Spectrum is a far more effective way to produce a cluster of frequencies around a set Center
> Frequency than by using any frequency wobble. **Wobbled frequencies are produced one after
> another (they are linear), and thus their dwell is momentary. Spectrum frequencies are produced
> in parallel, and their dwells are thus all equal to the Center Frequency's dwell.**"

Definitions opened: **MOR / Center Frequency**, **MOR tolerance / Frequency Tolerance**,
**Wave Cycle Multiplier (WCM)** = number of child frequencies; because children are created both
above *and* below, "**the number of child frequencies you create will be twice the value you enter**."

## p186
**Spectrum formulas, verbatim:**
- `Frequency Spacing = Center Frequency × MOR tolerance`
- `Spectrum % = Wave Cycle Multiplier × 100 × Frequency Spacing ÷ Center Frequency`
- `Frequency Spacing (FS)` = distance between child frequencies.
- `Spectrum %` = the range within which frequencies are created — "Spectrum is always a percentage
  of your Center Frequency."

**Example 1:** want 1.5 MHz, MOR tolerance 0.025%, 100 child frequencies → Center = 1,500,000 Hz,
MOR tolerance 0.00025, **WCM = 50**; "There are 101 frequencies in all, our *Center Frequency*, plus
50 below it and 50 above it." FS = 1,500,000 × 0.00025 = **375 Hz**.

## p187
Example 1 result: `50 × 100 × 375 ÷ 1,500,000 = 1.25` → **Spectrum % = 1.25**.
**Example 2:** 500 Hz, MOR tolerance 0.025%, 20 individual frequencies → Center 500 Hz,
MOR tolerance 0.00025, **WCM = 10**; FS = 500 × 0.00025 = **0.125 Hz**;
`10 × 100 × 0.125 ÷ 500 = 0.25` → **Spectrum % = 0.25**.
